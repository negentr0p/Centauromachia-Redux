Takedown requests

If you believe an image on this site should not be here, for example because it contains your work or your likeness, or because of another right or concern, please get in touch and the request will be reviewed.

What to send

  • The image ID (shown in the image’s details, for example ngntrp_20261012_00) or the address of the page it appears on.
  • What your concern is and, if it relates to a work or a right you hold, what that is.
  • How to reply to you.

Email: negentrop.corps [at] gmail.com

What happens next

You will receive a reply confirming the request was received. If it is upheld, the image is removed from this site and the reply will say what was done.

Training data

The model was trained on the artist’s own paintings and, from LoRA v3, a small set of public-domain and CC0 images from PD12M. If you think a work was included in error, say so in the same way. PD12M also has its own takedown process, run by its maintainers.

The images on this site are AI-generated (Stable Diffusion XL with the NGNTRP LoRA) and released under CC0 1.0 unless an image says otherwise.
